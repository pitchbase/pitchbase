Privacy

What we collect.

The PitchBase website does not ask you to create an account, does not use advertising or analytics tools, and does not set tracking cookies.

Payments.

Payments are processed by Payhip. When you subscribe, Payhip collects your name, email address and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your order details and email address from Payhip so we can provide access and answer support requests. We never see or store your full card number.

Hosting.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record basic technical data, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service.

Email.

If you write to us, we use your message and email address only to reply to you. We do not sell or share your information.

Your choices.

To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it, email pitchbase.support@proton.me.
