Canton Fair (China Import and Export Fair) — Phase 1

Electronics and manufacturing

Get badge · Claim

Guangzhou, Guangdong, ChinaPhase 1 of the twice-yearly Canton Fair, held at the Canton Fair Complex in Pazhou, Guangzhou. Organiser: China Foreign Trade Centre, as named in the CBD Guangzhou organiser note. Phase 1 scope: electronics and home appliances, industrial automation and manufacturing, vehicles and two-wheelers, lighting and electrical products, hardware and tools, and new energy. Scale stated for the 140th session: 1.55 million sqm for the full session, with the 139th session (spring 2026) cited in guides at 314,000 overseas buyers from 220 countries and regions and 32,000+ exhibitors. Edition status at snapshot 2026-10-29: the 140th session Phase 1 ran 15-19 October 2026 and has closed; do not present those dates as upcoming. Registration and visa (guide-level, re-check the official site before travel): overseas buyers pre-register through the official Buyer Service System, obtain a free overseas buyer badge, and can request an invitation letter in the same system to support a China business (M) visa application if their nationality requires one. A fair badge is not entry approval and an invitation letter does not guarantee a visa; visa-free eligibility varies by nationality, so check the Chinese embassy or consulate for the traveller&#x27;s passport.
