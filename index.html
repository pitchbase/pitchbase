Methodology

Every entry comes from a public source and carries a snapshot date. The directory is refreshed daily, and every field page shows its last update date — so what you see is current, not a frozen list. The advantage is coverage plus verification: searching China-side sources across languages and platforms is slow work, and checking what you find from abroad is slower. This directory does that gathering and checking from public sources. No private emails, phone numbers, or WeChat IDs are included, no paywalled content is scraped, and no guess is presented as fact. Entries are gathered by AI researchers from public sources and checked against fixed rules (source resolution, score recomputation, sensitive-data scan) before publication; this is not fully automatic verification. Re-confirm details with the firm or supplier before paying.

PitchBase score

PitchBase score is our own composite, not any platform's original rating. It is computed only from signals stated on the cited public page: years in operation (25 points for 10+ years, 15 for 5–9 years, 10 for 1–4 years), platform rating (25 for 4.8 and above, 20 for 4.5–4.7, 15 for 4.0–4.4, 10 below 4.0), certification, listing status, or other stated public credentials (25 when stated), and export evidence (25 when stated export activity, such as destinations or export share). Each scored entry shows its factor breakdown. Only factors with stated data count toward the total: the score is points earned divided by points available, scaled to 100. Entries with fewer than two stated signals show 'Not yet scored' and are never given a forced low score. Transaction tier and response speed are not included, because public sources do not systematically provide them. A founded year is never converted into years in operation, a platform membership badge ('X yrs') is never counted as years in operation, and client-tier statements (for example, 'Fortune 500 experience') do not count as export evidence. The Investment field has no score column: it has no corresponding public signals. A platform's original rating stays visible as its own field wherever it exists.
