Complaints and suggestions (private)

If something is wrong with a file, a listing, or our service, tell us privately. Complaints and suggestions are not published on this site and are not visible to other visitors. Only our support team reads them.

How to reach us privately: buyers — use the order route on Payhip (open your order and contact us from there). Before you buy — send us a private message through the store's existing inbound channels (Payhip / social private messages).

Please include, when relevant: your order number (buyers), the product name, what is wrong, and what outcome you are asking for. Do not include passwords, card numbers, verification codes, or bank details in your message — we never ask for them.

What happens next: we review every private complaint and suggestion. Accuracy reports (a listing that is wrong or out of date) are checked against public sources; confirmed corrections go into a later update. Refunds, discounts, and free full files are not decided by support alone; they are passed to the owner for a decision. We do not promise a sourcing, funding, partnership, or sales outcome, and a complaint does not change that.

If anyone contacts you claiming to be us and asks for a direct transfer, a password, a card number, or a verification code, it is not us. Stop and reach us again through the order route above.
