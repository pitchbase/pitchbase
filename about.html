About

PitchBase is a curated directory of Chinese suppliers, manufacturers and service teams for overseas buyers. We build it from public information, such as company websites, published portfolios and public business listings, and we organise it by field and region so you can find a starting point quickly.

Each entry shows the checks done and the date it was last verified, so confirm the details yourself before you place an order.

PitchBase is operated by the PitchBase team. We do not represent any company listed, and a listing is not an endorsement.
